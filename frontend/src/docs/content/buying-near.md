## Steps

1. Open the **Buy** tab.
2. Choose your currency and enter an amount, or tap a quick amount. You'll see about how much NEAR you get.
3. Tap **Buy NEAR**.
4. You'll get **transfer details**: bank, account number, account name and the exact amount.
5. From **a bank account in your own name**, send **exactly** that amount, in **one** transfer.
6. Tap **I've sent the money**.

When the payment is received, the NEAR is bought and lands in your Paya balance. You'll see **Received** in **Activity**.

> [!WARNING]
> Send the exact amount, in one transfer, from an account in your own name. A different amount or someone else's account can delay the order or need a refund through support.

## Good to know

- **Each order has its own account number.** Don't reuse transfer details from an earlier order.
- **Auto cash-out turns off when you buy**, so the NEAR you bought isn't sold straight back. Turn it on again any time.
- **Minimum purchase:** the app shows it under the amount. It's based on the live rate: about ₦8,000 today.

## Cancelling

If you haven't paid yet, tap **Cancel order**. Orders you haven't marked as paid close automatically after **2 hours**.

> [!TIP]
> If you paid an order that then closed, don't worry: payments made within 24 hours are still delivered.
