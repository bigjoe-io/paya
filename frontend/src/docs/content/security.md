## How your NEAR is held

Your NEAR address belongs to your account and nobody else's. Paya manages its key for you, which is why you don't need to connect a wallet or keep a seed phrase. Your NEAR only ever moves in three ways:

1. **To your own bank**, when you cash out.
2. **To another wallet**, when you send it and confirm with a code from your email.
3. **Back to your balance**, if an exchange can't complete.

## Protections

- **Email codes.** No passwords to steal. Sending NEAR anywhere except your bank needs a fresh code every time.
- **Verified bank.** Your bank account is confirmed with the bank, and payouts only go there.
- **Exact amounts.** Paya pays our payment partner exactly what arrived from the exchange, never more.
- **Refunds built in.** A failed exchange returns your NEAR to your balance automatically. A failed purchase is retried with another provider.
- **Every step on-chain.** Each order lists its NEAR and Solana transaction IDs, which anyone can check on a public explorer.

## Paya will never

- Ask for your sign-in code by phone, chat or email. **Never share it with anyone.**
- Ask you to send NEAR or money to "verify" or "unlock" your account.
- Ask for your bank PIN, card details or online-banking password.

> [!WARNING]
> If someone asks for your code, it's a scam. Don't share it, and contact support.

## If you lose access to your email

Your email is your login. Secure it with a strong password and two-step verification. If you lose access, contact support with your Support IDs; don't create a new account.
