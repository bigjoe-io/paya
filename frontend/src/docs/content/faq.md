## Do I need a crypto wallet?

No. Your Paya account comes with its own NEAR address. You can send NEAR to it from any wallet or exchange.

## Is my NEAR address permanent?

Yes. It belongs to your account and never changes, even if you change your bank.

## Can I have more than one bank account?

One payout bank at a time. You can change it whenever you like.

## How much does it cost?

A 0.5% Paya fee plus the exchange and payment partner rates, all included in the amount shown before you confirm. See [Fees and limits](/docs/fees-and-limits).

## How long does a cash-out take?

Usually 5–10 minutes.

## What if the exchange fails?

Your NEAR returns to your balance automatically, and the cash-out shows **Refunded**.

## Can I cancel a cash-out?

Not once it has started: the NEAR is already on its way. If it can't complete, it comes back to you automatically.

## Can I cancel a purchase?

Yes, before you pay: tap **Cancel order**.

## Is Paya a bank?

No. Paya is a technology platform. Bank payouts and collections are made by a licensed payment partner.
