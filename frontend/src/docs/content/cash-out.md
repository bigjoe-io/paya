## Two ways to cash out

### Automatic (default)

With **Auto cash-out** on, any NEAR that arrives at your address is paid to your bank, as long as there's at least **1 NEAR** to cash out. Turn it on or off on the **Sell** tab or the **Account** page.

### When you choose

Turn **Auto cash-out** off and NEAR stays in your balance. To cash out:

1. On the **Sell** tab, under **Cash out now**, enter an amount, or tap **Half** or **Max**.
2. Check what you'll receive. Tap the rate to see the full breakdown.
3. Tap **Cash out to NGN** (or your currency).

## The button tells you what's wrong

The button only works for an amount that will go through:

| Button says | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Minimum is 1 NEAR** | Enter at least 1 NEAR |
| **Max is … NEAR** | You entered more than you can send; 0.02 NEAR always stays for network fees |
| **Verify email to cash out** | Your payment session expired; verify your email again |
| **Cash out to NGN** | Ready |

## What happens next

You'll see **Processing payout** with a spinner. Behind the scenes:

1. Your NEAR goes from your address to an exchange provider.
2. The provider converts it to USDC (a digital US dollar).
3. Paya passes exactly that USDC to our payment partner.
4. The partner pays your bank in your local currency.

When it's done the screen shows **Paid**. You can close the screen at any time; it keeps going.

## How long it takes

Usually **5–10 minutes**. Now and then a provider takes longer. If it takes more than 10 minutes, the app says so.

## If it can't complete

If the exchange can't be completed, your NEAR is **returned to your Paya balance** automatically and the cash-out shows **Returned to your balance**. You can try again.

> [!NOTE]
> The amount on screen before you confirm is an estimate. The final amount is set when the USDC is received, a few minutes later. It's usually within a few percent of the estimate.
