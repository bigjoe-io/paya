## The cash-out button is greyed out

The button explains why: **Minimum is 1 NEAR**, **Max is … NEAR**, or **Verify email to cash out**. Adjust the amount, or verify your email again.

## "Processing payout" is taking a while

Most cash-outs finish in 5–10 minutes. A provider can occasionally take longer. You can close the app; it keeps going. If an exchange can't complete, your NEAR comes back to your balance automatically. After an hour, contact support with the Support ID.

## I sent NEAR but my balance didn't change

- Check you sent it on the **NEAR** network, to the exact address on your Sell tab.
- Exchanges can take a few minutes to send. Check the transaction in your exchange's history.
- With **Auto cash-out** on, NEAR is paid out as soon as it arrives, so your balance may go straight back down. Check **Activity**.

## My NEAR didn't cash out automatically

- There must be at least **1 NEAR** to cash out.
- Buying NEAR turns Auto cash-out off. Turn it back on on the Sell tab.
- If you see **Verify again**, verify your email: the payout waits until you do.

## The bank name check failed

Check the account number and bank, then try again in a few minutes. See [Link or change your bank](/docs/linking-a-bank).

## I paid for a purchase but nothing happened

Make sure you tapped **I've sent the money**. Transfers can take a few minutes to reach our payment partner. If it's been over an hour, contact support with the Support ID and your bank's transfer receipt.

## I sent the wrong amount for a purchase

Contact support with the Support ID and your transfer receipt. Don't send a second transfer to make up the difference.

## I'm asked for my BVN or NIN

Our payment partner sometimes needs a one-time identity check. Enter it in the form Paya shows. Paya doesn't store the number.
