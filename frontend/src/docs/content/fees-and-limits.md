## Fees

| | Cost | Who it goes to |
|---|---|---|
| **Paya fee** | 0.5% of the dollar value | Paya |
| **Exchange rate** | Each provider's rate includes a small margin and a fixed cost of about $0.45 | The exchange provider |
| **Payout or collection rate** | Our payment partner's live local-currency rate | The payment partner |
| **NEAR network fee** | Fractions of a cent, paid from the 0.02 NEAR kept in your balance | NEAR network |

Every fee is already included in the amount you see before you confirm. Tap the rate on the **Sell** or **Buy** screen for the breakdown.

> [!TIP]
> The exchange's fixed cost matters less on bigger amounts. Cashing out 10 NEAR at once costs a smaller share than ten 1 NEAR cash-outs.

## Limits

| | Limit |
|---|---|
| Smallest cash-out | **1 NEAR** (and at least about $2.50 paid out) |
| Largest cash-out | Your balance minus 0.02 NEAR |
| Smallest purchase | Shown in the app, from the live rate: about ₦8,000 today |
| Kept for network fees | 0.02 NEAR |

Minimums move with exchange rates; the app always shows the current figure.
