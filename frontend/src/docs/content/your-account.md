## One account, three things

| | |
|---|---|
| **Your email** | How you sign in. There's no password: you get a one-time code each time. |
| **Your NEAR address** | A 64-character address that belongs to your account only. It never changes. |
| **Your payout bank** | Where cash-outs are paid. One at a time; you can change it. |

You'll find all three on the **Account** page.

## Signing in

Enter your email, then the code we send. On the same device you stay signed in for 30 days. Signing in on a new device gives you the same account, address and balance.

## "Verify again"

Cash-outs and purchases use a session with our payment partner that expires from time to time. When it does, you'll see **Verify again** or a button like **Verify email to cash out**. Tap it, enter the new code, and carry on. Your balance and settings don't change.

> [!NOTE]
> If a cash-out reaches the payout step while your session has expired, the money waits safely with Paya and is paid as soon as you verify again.

## Your balance

**Balance** is the NEAR at your address. A small amount (0.02 NEAR) always stays there to pay network fees, so the most you can cash out or send is slightly less than your balance. The app shows the exact maximum.

## Hide balances

Tap the eye icon at the top to hide amounts on screen. It only affects this device.

## Signing out

**Account → Sign out**. Your account and funds are not affected; sign in again any time with your email.
