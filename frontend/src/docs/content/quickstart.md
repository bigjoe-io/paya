## Step 1: Sign in with your email

1. Open the app and tap **Get started** (or **Sign in**).
2. Enter your email and tap **Send code**.
3. Enter the code from the email.

That's it: your account and your own NEAR address are created the moment you verify. The code comes from our payment partner, PAJ.

## Step 2: Link your bank account

1. Choose your country.
2. Tap **Link bank account**, then search for your bank by name. Typing `gtb`, `uba` or `zenith` works.
3. Type your account number. Paya checks it with the bank and shows the account holder's name.
4. If the name is right, tap **Save and use**.

> [!TIP]
> Only link an account in your own name. The name shown is the one the bank has on file.

## Step 3: Copy your NEAR address

On the **Sell** tab you'll see **Your NEAR address**: a 64-character code. Tap **Copy**, or the QR icon to show it as a QR code.

## Step 4: Send NEAR to it

From any NEAR wallet or exchange, send NEAR to your address. Choose the **NEAR** network when your exchange asks.

## Step 5: Get paid

With **Auto cash-out** on (the default), NEAR that arrives is converted and paid to your bank automatically. You'll see **Processing payout**, then **Paid**. Most cash-outs take 5–10 minutes.

Prefer to choose when? Turn **Auto cash-out** off and use **Cash out now** instead. See [Cash out](/docs/cash-out).

> [!NOTE]
> The smallest cash-out is **1 NEAR**. Smaller amounts stay in your balance until there's enough.
