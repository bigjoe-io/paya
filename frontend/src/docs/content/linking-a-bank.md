## Link your bank

1. Go to **Account → Payout bank** (or the prompt on the main screen).
2. Choose your country.
3. Tap the bank field and type part of your bank's name. Initials work too: `gtb` finds Guaranty Trust Bank, `uba` finds United Bank for Africa.
4. Type your account number. As soon as it's complete, Paya checks it with the bank. The button spins while it checks.
5. Check the name that appears, then tap **Save and use**.

## Why the name check matters

The name comes from the bank, not from what you type. If it isn't your name, don't save it: you'd be sending money to someone else's account.

## If the check fails

- Double-check the account number and the bank you picked.
- Some banks are briefly unavailable; try again in a few minutes.
- If the bank isn't in the list, it isn't supported yet in that country.

## Change your bank

**Account → Payout bank → Change bank account**, then follow the same steps. Future cash-outs go to the new account. Cash-outs already in progress still go to the account they started with.
