## Steps

1. Go to **Account → Send NEAR**.
2. Enter the address: a name like `alice.near`, or a 64-character address.
3. Enter the amount, or tap **Max**.
4. Tap **Continue**. We email you a code.
5. Enter the code and tap **Confirm and send**.

It arrives in seconds. You'll get a link to the transaction.

## Why a code every time

Sending NEAR anywhere other than your own bank always needs a fresh code from your email. Even someone using your unlocked phone can't send your NEAR elsewhere without your email.

## Before you send

- **Check the address carefully.** NEAR transfers can't be reversed.
- **Sending to an exchange?** Use your exchange's **NEAR network** deposit address. If the exchange asks for a memo for NEAR deposits, check with them first: Paya doesn't add memos.
- Names like `alice.near` must already exist. Paya checks and stops you if they don't.
