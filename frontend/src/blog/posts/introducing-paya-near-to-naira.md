Across Africa, more people and businesses are getting paid in crypto every month — freelancers, developers, creators, online shops. But rent, salaries and suppliers are still paid in local money. Moving between the two has been the hard part.

Today the usual route looks like this: send your crypto to an exchange, sell it, find a P2P buyer, wait for them to pay, then confirm. It takes time, it costs more than it should, and every step is a chance for something to go wrong.

**Paya makes it one step.** It's an app that moves money between NEAR and your bank account, in both directions.

## Cash out NEAR to your bank in naira

Every Paya account comes with its own NEAR address. Share it like a bank account number — with clients, customers or your own exchange — and anything sent to it shows up in your Paya balance.

When you want naira, open **Sell**, choose an amount or tap **Max**, and Paya does the rest. It converts your NEAR and pays out to your linked Nigerian bank account. Before you confirm, you see exactly how much naira will land, at what rate, and every fee.

Payouts usually arrive within minutes, and you can follow every step in the app.

## Or let it happen automatically

Turn on **auto cash-out** and you never have to open the app. Every time NEAR arrives at your Paya address, it's converted and paid to your bank account automatically.

For a business, that changes what accepting crypto means. A customer pays in NEAR; a few minutes later, naira is in your account. No one on your team has to manage a wallet, watch prices or run a P2P trade.

## Buy NEAR with a bank transfer

It works the other way too. Type how much naira you want to spend, see exactly how much NEAR you'll get, and pay with a normal bank transfer. The NEAR lands in your balance a few minutes later. Here's [the full guide to buying NEAR in Nigeria](/blog/how-to-buy-near-in-nigeria).

## Built for people, not crypto experts

**Sign in with your email.** There's no seed phrase to write down and no browser extension to install. We send a one-time code to your inbox, and that's how you sign in.

**Your money stays traceable.** Every purchase and payout is listed in Activity with its transaction IDs on the NEAR and Solana blockchains and its bank reference. Your accountant will thank you.

**Clear, simple fees.** Paya charges 0.5%. The rate, fees and the exact amount you'll receive are on screen before you confirm.

**Sending needs your inbox.** To send NEAR out of Paya, you confirm with a code we email you, so nobody can move your money without access to your email.

## How it works under the hood

When you cash out, Paya swaps your NEAR into USDC, a dollar stablecoin, and our payout partner pays the naira into your bank account. When you buy, the same route runs in reverse: your bank transfer becomes USDC, which is swapped into NEAR and delivered to your address.

You don't need to know any of that to use Paya — but it means every step is recorded on public blockchains and can be checked.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I convert NEAR to naira?

Send NEAR to your Paya address, open Sell, choose an amount and confirm. Paya converts it and pays naira to your linked Nigerian bank account, usually within minutes.

### Can I cash out NEAR to a Nigerian bank account automatically?

Yes. Turn on auto cash-out and every NEAR payment that arrives at your Paya address is converted and sent to your bank account without you opening the app.

### What's the minimum I can cash out?

The minimum cash-out is 1 NEAR. A tiny amount of NEAR always stays in your balance to cover network fees.

### Is Paya a crypto exchange?

No. Paya isn't an order book or a P2P market. It's a direct route between NEAR and your bank account: you see one price, confirm once, and the money moves.
