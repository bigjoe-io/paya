If you've tried to buy NEAR in Nigeria, you know the usual routes. You sign up on a big exchange, wait for verification, find a P2P trader, send naira to a stranger and hope they release your coins. Then you withdraw to a wallet and copy a long address without a typo.

Paya replaces all of that with one bank transfer. You type how much naira you want to spend, Paya shows you exactly how much NEAR you'll get, and you pay from your own bank account like any other transfer. The NEAR lands in your Paya balance a few minutes later.

This guide walks through it step by step.

## What you need

- **An email address.** That's your sign-in. Paya sends you a one-time code; there's no password and no seed phrase to write down.
- **A Nigerian bank account in your own name.** You'll send the naira from it. Any bank or fintech app that can make a normal transfer works.
- **A few minutes.**

You don't need an exchange account, a separate crypto wallet or any NEAR to start with.

## Step 1: Sign in with your email

Open [usepaya.xyz](https://www.usepaya.xyz) and sign in with your email. Enter the code we send you and you're in.

The first time you sign in, Paya creates your own NEAR address. It's a real address on the NEAR blockchain — you can receive NEAR on it from any wallet or exchange, and you can see it in the app at any time.

## Step 2: Enter how much naira you want to spend

Open **Buy** and type an amount in naira, or tap one of the quick amounts.

Before you pay anything, Paya shows you:

- **How much NEAR you get**, at the live rate.
- **The rate** you're getting, in naira per NEAR.
- **Every fee**, line by line: Paya's 0.5% fee and the estimated network fee.
- **The minimum you'll receive**, even if the price moves while your order is processed.

No surprises later. What you see is what lands in your balance.

## Step 3: Make the bank transfer

When you tap **Buy NEAR**, Paya gives you the account details to pay into and the exact amount. Open your banking app and send a normal transfer.

Two things matter here:

1. **Send the exact amount shown.** A different amount can delay your order.
2. **Pay from an account in your own name.** Transfers from someone else's account can be held for checks.

## Step 4: Your NEAR arrives

As soon as your transfer lands, Paya converts it and sends the NEAR to your address. It usually takes a few minutes after your transfer, and the app shows each step as it happens.

Every purchase appears in **Activity** with its transaction IDs, so you can always trace exactly where your money went — from your bank transfer to the NEAR in your balance.
